---
w3Develops brings developers together to learn and build: study groups, group projects, mentorship, pair programming, meetups, competitions, a job board and tech news. Everything is free.

- **[Get started](/docs/quickstart)**: Make an account and join your first group.
- **[Study groups](/docs/guides/study-groups)**: Learn a language or framework with others, on a schedule.
- **[Community feed](/docs/guides/community-feed)**: Share what you're learning and building.
- **[Contribute](/docs/contributing/overview)**: Help build w3develops.org. It's open source.

## Principles

- **Free to use.** Learning and collaborating cost nothing.
- **Learn by building.** Groups end with something real: a project, a portfolio, a habit.
- **Be kind.** Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](https://w3develops.org/code-of-conduct).
