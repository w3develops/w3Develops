The [community feed](https://w3develops.org/feed) is for short posts: progress, questions, wins and useful links.

## Posting

- Posts are up to 500 characters, with up to 3 topics, an optional link and an optional image.
- You can post 5 times an hour and reply 30 times an hour.
- Mention someone with `@username` to notify them.
- Choose who sees each post: **Public** or **Followers**.

## Private accounts

Turn on **Private account** in the "Your feed" card. Your posts become followers-only, and new people send a follow request that you accept or decline.

## Likes, replies and following

Like and reply to posts, follow members and topics, and mute or block people you don't want to see. Posts from people and topics you follow come first.

> Posts and replies go through an automatic safety check and a blocked-word list. Hidden posts can be appealed. See [Safety and reporting](/docs/guides/safety).
