---
title: For moderators
description: Reviewing reports and keeping the community safe.
---
Moderators work on [/moderation](https://w3develops.org/moderation). Every action is saved in the log.

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Reported posts | Posts and replies with open reports, including ones the automatic check hid |
| Member reports | Reports about profiles and listings |
| Appeals | Members asking for another look; answer each one |
| Hidden | Other hidden posts |
| Writing | Writer applications and articles waiting to be published |
| Log | Every moderation action |
| Team | Add or remove moderators (admins only) |

Moderators also see edit and delete controls on members' groups, meetups, competitions, jobs, projects, listings and organizations. New reports and appeals arrive as notifications in the bell.

Act kindly and briefly, and follow the [Code of Conduct](https://w3develops.org/code-of-conduct).
