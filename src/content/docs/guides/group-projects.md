## Group projects

[Group projects](https://w3develops.org/groupprojects) are teams building one application together, which gives you Git, teamwork and portfolio experience. They have the same check-ins and tasks as study groups. Start one at [/groupprojects/create](https://w3develops.org/groupprojects/create).

## Solo projects

Showcase something you built on [Solo Projects](https://w3develops.org/solo-projects). Members can star projects they like. Your own projects have a private-to-edit task list under **Your tasks for this project**.

## Need an idea?

[Project ideas](https://w3develops.org/project-ideas) lists projects by level and track, and marks the ones that suit a team.
