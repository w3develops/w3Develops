## Services directory

On the [services directory](https://w3develops.org/marketplace), members list up to five services they offer, like websites, design or tutoring. Listing and browsing are free, and people contact you through your profile. w3Develops doesn't take a cut or handle payments, so agree on the work and price before starting.

## Organizations

[Organizations](https://w3develops.org/orgs) are pages for the businesses, nonprofits, communities and teams members run. The owner chooses admins; others ask to join and admins accept them.

## Writing for w3Develops

Members can [apply to write](https://w3develops.org/news/write) tutorials and stories for the news page. Moderators review each article before it's published.
