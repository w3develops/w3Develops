---
title: Quickstart
description: From sign-up to your first group in a few minutes.
---
1. **Make an account.** [Sign up](https://w3develops.org/signup) with Google, GitHub or your email, and verify your email address. Some things, like posting, need a verified email.
2. **Fill in your profile.** Add a bio, your skills and links on your [profile](https://w3develops.org/profile/edit). Profiles are public, so others can find people to work with.
3. **Join a group.** Pick a [study group](https://w3develops.org/studygroups), [group project](https://w3develops.org/groupprojects) or [book club](https://w3develops.org/book-clubs), or start your own.
4. **Say hello.** Introduce yourself on the [community feed](https://w3develops.org/feed) and in our [Discord](https://w3develops.org/chat).

> Not sure what to build? Browse [project ideas](https://w3develops.org/project-ideas) or try today's [Algorithms Daily](https://w3develops.org/algorithms-daily) problem.
