## Principles

- **Free to use.** Learning and collaborating cost nothing.
- **Learn by building.** Groups end with something real: a project, a portfolio, a habit.
- **Be kind.** Everyone follows the [Code of Conduct](https://w3develops.org/code-of-conduct).
