## Report

Use **Report** on a profile, post, reply or listing. Reports are private and go to the moderation team. A post with reports from three different members is hidden until a moderator reviews it.

## Mute and block

- **Mute** hides someone's posts from your feed. They aren't told.
- **Block** also removes them from your followers and stops them following you or replying to your posts.

## Automatic checks

New posts and replies are checked against a blocked-word list and an automatic text and image check. Anything flagged is hidden until a moderator looks at it.

## Appeals

If a moderator hides something of yours, warns you or pauses your posting, select **Appeal** to ask for another look. You'll get the answer in your notifications.
