---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "From sign-up to your first group in a few minutes."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make an account">
    [Sign up](https://w3develops.org/signup) with Google, GitHub or your email, and verify your email address. Some things, like posting, need a verified email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your profile">
    Add a bio, your skills and links on your [profile](https://w3develops.org/profile/edit). Profiles are public, so others can find people to work with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join a group">
    Pick a [study group](https://w3develops.org/studygroups), [group project](https://w3develops.org/groupprojects) or [book club](https://w3develops.org/book-clubs), or start your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say hello">
    Introduce yourself on the [community feed](https://w3develops.org/feed) and in our [Discord](https://w3develops.org/chat).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Not sure what to build? Browse [project ideas](https://w3develops.org/project-ideas) or try today's [Algorithms Daily](https://w3develops.org/algorithms-daily) problem.</Tip>
